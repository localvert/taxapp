Sources & Assumptions

Every figure below traces to one of the two boxes right below, or is called out inline as a modeling assumption. Tap a section to expand it — start with whichever jurisdiction or question you're trying to double-check.

⚠ California figures are confirmed 2025 numbers, not yet 2026 — the FTB hadn't published official 2026 brackets/thresholds at build time. The "CA inflation adjustment" input on the left lets you approximate 2026 by scaling the 2025 figures; leave it at 0 to see 2025 numbers as-is.
Federal — confirmed 2026 figures
  • Ordinary brackets, standard deduction, LTCG breakpoints, AMT exemption/phase-out/rate breakpoint: IRS Rev. Proc. 2025-32; summarized in IR-2025-103. AMT phase-out threshold ($500K single / $1M MFJ) and 50% phase-out rate reflect OBBBA §70107 amending IRC §55(d) — a real tightening vs. pre-2026 law (the phase-out rate doubled from 25% to 50%, and the threshold dropped roughly back to pre-TCJA levels). This is the single biggest driver of high marginal rates on ISO exercises and large capital gains this year — see the Marginal Rate section for where it bites.
  • NIIT (3.8%, $200K/$250K) and Additional Medicare Tax (0.9%, $200K/$250K): IRC §1411 / §3101(b)(2) — fixed statutory dollar thresholds, not inflation-indexed, unchanged by OBBBA.
  • 2026 Social Security wage base ($184,500) and employee-side rate (6.2%): confirmed via SSA's October 2025 announcement.
  • SALT cap ($40,400, phase-out starting $505,000 MAGI at 30¢/$, floor $10,000): OBBBA amendment to IRC §164(b)(6).
  • "2/37" itemized deduction limitation for top-bracket filers (new IRC §68, replacing the repealed Pease limitation): reduces itemized deductions by 2/37 of the lesser of itemized deductions or income above the 37%-bracket threshold — caps deduction value at 35¢/$ instead of 37¢/$.
  • Charitable AGI limits (60% cash / 30% appreciated stock, both to public charities/DAFs), 5-year carryforward: IRC §170(b), IRS Pub. 526.
  • New 0.5%-of-AGI charitable floor (new IRC §170(b)(1)(L), effective 2026): first 0.5% of AGI given is nondeductible. assumption — this model applies the floor against cash contributions before stock; the statute's ordering wasn't explicit in publicly available guidance at build time, so this is a modeling choice, not a sourced rule.
  • ISO AMT preference item (bargain element added to AMTI in exercise year) and minimum tax credit carryforward: IRC §56(b)(3); IRS Form 6251 & Form 8801 instructions.
California — confirmed 2025 figures, 2026 projected
  • Tax brackets (Schedule X/Y): 2025 CA Tax Rate Schedules.
  • Standard deduction ($5,706 single / $11,412 MFJ): FTB Deductions.
  • Mental Health Services Tax (1% over $1,000,000): R&TC §17043 (Prop. 63). The $1M threshold is a fixed statutory amount, not inflation-indexed — it stays $1,000,000 regardless of the CA inflation adjustment input.
  • CA AMT (7% flat rate, exemption $92,749 single/$123,667 MFJ, 25% phase-out starting $347,808/$463,745): 2025 Instructions for Schedule P (540). CA conforms to federal ISO preference-item treatment, but its phase-out rate stayed at the original 25% — CA's static conformity date (IRC as of Jan 1, 2015) predates OBBBA's 2026 federal change to 50%, so CA's AMT "hump" is noticeably milder than the new federal one.
  • CA high-income itemized deduction limitation (reduces itemized deductions by lesser of 6% of AGI above $237,035 single/$474,075 MFJ, or 80% of itemized deductions) — CA never repealed its Pease-style limitation the way federal law did: 2025 Instructions for Schedule CA (540).
  • CA charitable cash-gift limit is 50% of AGI, not 60% — CA's static conformity date predates the TCJA change that raised the federal cash limit to 60%. Confirmed directly in the Schedule CA (540) instructions. Appreciated-stock limit remains 30% (unchanged federal vs. CA). CA does not apply the new federal 0.5% floor.
  • CA SDI rate 1.3% for 2026, no wage base cap (removed by SB 951 effective 2024): confirmed via EDD Rates & Withholding and independently cross-checked against 2026 SDI-rate coverage.
  • 2026 CA brackets/standard deduction/AMT exemption were not yet published by FTB at build time (typically released with the following year's booklet, around Nov/Dec). This model uses confirmed 2025 numbers, adjustable via the inflation input (recent CA CCPI adjustments have run ~3%/year) — treat any non-zero value as a rough projection, not an official figure. SDI (1.3%) and the MHST $1M threshold above are confirmed 2026 numbers regardless, since they're set independently of the CCPI bracket cycle.
Simplifying assumptions
  • No above-the-line adjustments (401(k), HSA, etc.) are modeled — all income inputs are treated as already net of any pre-tax deferrals. AGI = total income entered.
  • Mortgage interest is not modeled as an itemized deduction (add manually to the property-tax field as a rough proxy if needed — this will slightly overstate the SALT/property-tax category, so use with care).
  • Federal SALT-deduction "tax paid" is proxied by this model's own computed CA liability for the same year (plus property tax), not actual withholding/estimated payments — a common simplification, but real-world timing can differ.
  • ISOs are assumed exercised-and-held past year-end (qualifying disposition path) — no same-year disqualifying-disposition scenario is modeled.
  • Capital loss carryforwards (beyond netting entered directly in the capital-gains events), QBI deduction, other credits (child tax credit, foreign tax credit, etc.), and multi-year AMT credit (Form 8801/FTB 3510) carryforward are out of scope for this single-year model.
  • NIIT/AMT/itemized-limitation interactions are all computed from first principles per the statute/worksheet mechanics described above, not looked up from a table — verified against several hand-calculated scenarios during build but not a substitute for CPA review, especially near threshold "cliffs" like the AMT phase-out band.
